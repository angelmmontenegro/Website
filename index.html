You know what Ronnie Fieg, Pat Riley, and JFK Jr. had in common? They were all fans of Giorgio Armani.

I started looking through runway photos, campaigns, editorials, and more of his work, and I’m absolutely obsessed. I never realized that his work is the type of clothing I really like. It matches the style I already have and want to keep drawing influence from.

This is just a thought, but I really appreciate a middle ground between formal and business casual. I think his work, especially in the ’90s, is the epitome of that balance. I want my style to go in that direction.

In conclusion: ’90s Helmut Lang, Jil Sander, Giorgio Armani, and today’s cool clothes are the direction I’m going.
